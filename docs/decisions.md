## Open questions

- **Early game too slow at the current scale.** Refinery level 2 takes 54 real minutes, while the design document plans two very fast first hours. To be fixed in week 17 with a time curve specific to the first levels.
- **The other buildings pass the storage from level 21 to 23.** They keep ×1.6 a level, while the storage follows the Lighthouse (×1.3 past level 15): even at Lighthouse 30 with Sealed Warehouses 3 (2,597,893), upgrading the Barracks, Harbor or Observatory from level 21 costs more than the storage holds, the Refinery, Shelters or Infirmary from 22, the Fishery or Lumberyard from 23 (one level sooner without the Warehouses). The owner first saw 24 to 26, measured with the steeper curve before this one. The players of the simulation are at level 14 to 18 after a year. To fix before players get there.
- **Oil overflows.** The regular and dedicated players' oil is full 651 and 1,103 hours in their first 90 days (1,331 and 4,201 in a year): salvaging wrecks brings more than anything asks. To give it a use in week 7 (the Mist's stoking is the first candidate).
- **When fish reaches 0 during a long absence, nothing else happens yet (survivors stay, morale does not drop).** To decide in week 17.
- **A wreck refills at once when emptied: add a delay?** To decide in week 17.
- **Camp levels are per player in the client; the server will own them in multiplayer.**
- **The CI's actions `actions/checkout`, `actions/setup-node` and `actions/upload-artifact` are at v4, which runs on Node 20, deprecated by GitHub.** To move to their next major version later, not now.
- **Two tabs offline at the same time share the account's copy on this device.** If one is closed before the network comes back, its waiting actions can be lost (actions already sent are safe, thanks to their ids). To look at if players use several tabs.
