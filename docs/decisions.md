Action `research` (`tech`, a known research). The Observatory sheet lists the 10 research as in the prototype; the Veteran Doctrine says in one sentence that it takes effect once troops can be trained (week 5). It works without an account too. | The rules already held the effects; the server decides research as it decides upgrades. |
| 2026-10-16 | The city changes shape for the first time (`research`, then `yard` for rafts). `parseState` requires the new fields; `migrateSave` adds them, empty, to old local saves without an account, which are never set aside for it. The e2e harness can put a city for a test account straight into its own database (`e2e/harness.ts`), never through the server. | A player without an account keeps their city; nothing exists in the server to write a city from outside. |
| 2026-10-16 | The cheat test (`server/test/cheat.test.ts`, week 4, task 5) keeps a reference city beside the server, made with the shared rules alone (`catchUp` on the server's real time, then `applyAction` for each accepted action), and checks the server's city against it after every case. The cases: an upgrade without resources, above the Lighthouse or with both builders busy; a fleet without troops, to an unknown site, or beyond the Harbor's fleets; one id replayed ten times; an unknown type, extra fields (a city, a time, a speed, another player's id, `__proto__`) and a body over 2 KB; an action id already used by another player (applied for this player, as their own); a client with an edited city; a burst of actions from a client with a faster clock. One end-to-end scenario does the same from a real page: city edited, clocks 1000 times faster, actions sent by hand. | Checkpoint of phase 2: a modified client can neither create resources nor speed up time. |
| 2026-10-16 | `parseState` is stricter: levels (buildings 0 to 30, research up to `TECHS[id].max`, captains 1 to `CAPTAIN_MAX_LEVEL`, rafts up to `RAFT_BUILDING_MAX_LEVEL`), troops, survivors, Pearls and report levels are whole numbers, and nothing is below 0; resources (stock, cargo, loot, wrecks) and morale keep their decimals, since production runs by the second. A research or captain missing from the city is level 0 or not owned yet, as the rules read them: unknown ids are refused, but no id is required. A raft must sit on a known slot (`RAFT_SLOTS`), with a known building or none and a condition from 0 to 100. A long scripted game, with time cut at odd lengths, checks that the rules never give a decimal where a whole number is required. `GET /city` and the 400 answer to an invalid action check the city caught up by `cityAt` too: refused, nothing is sent, the answer is `server_error`, and the log has the player id only. | Since week 4, `parseState` guards what the server writes and sends; adding a research or a captain later must not make the stored cities unreadable. |
| 2026-10-16 | `PUT /city` is removed, with `readSentCity` (`server/src/city/check.ts`), `createCity`, `saveCity`, their tests and their refusal codes (`version_conflict`, `invalid_city`, `city_too_large`, `too_many_saves`); the server accepts only `GET` and `POST` from the game's pages. `GET /city` makes the city of a new account at its first call (`ensureCity`, the same function as the actions); after that, reading stores nothing. | The server decides: a city changes only through actions checked with the shared rules. Ends the week 3 exception. |
| 2026-10-16 | Signed in, the client sends actions (week 4, task 4): each one is applied at once on the city shown, then sent, one at a time and in order (the next leaves with the answer to the previous one), with `keepalive`. The city shown is the server's last city, advanced by the time since its answer (`performance.now()`) at the server's `speed`, with the waiting actions applied on top with `applyAction`: an answer replaces only the server's part, so a waiting upgrade never disappears and comes back. No answer (no network, nothing within 10 s) or 429: the same action, same id, after 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, then 30 s, and at once when the network is back or the page is shown again. 422: the server's city, the next actions applied on it, a toast with `error`. 400, 409 `action_id_reused`, 413: the action is dropped and written to the console, the server's city is taken. `server_error` or an unknown code: as no answer; the action is dropped only after 3 minutes of errors in a row (a missing answer ends the run), then the console, `GET /city`, a toast. `unreadable_city`: nothing more is sent. At most 20 actions wait ("You are offline. Reconnect to keep playing."). An action sent without an answer more than 6 days ago is dropped (the server forgets ids after 7, so it could be applied twice); one never sent is kept, however old. | The screen reacts at once and the server decides; nothing the server did not refuse is lost to a lost network, and a database restart of a few minutes never loses an action. One action at a time keeps the order the player chose. |
| 2026-10-16 | While an action waits for its answer, the city shown applies it at the current moment: an upgrade's countdown does not move until the server has it. Wanted. | The server starts the upgrade when it receives it, so the countdown shown is the true one: less than a second online, frozen while offline. |
| 2026-10-16 | On the device, the city played without an account stays under `brinelight-client-v1`; each account has its own copy, `brinelight-account-city-v1:<player id>` (the server's last city, when it arrived, and the waiting actions with the time of their first send), only ever sent with that player's token, and kept when another account signs in. The session (`brinelight-account-v1`) is `{ token, wallet, playerId }`, the id coming from the `/auth/verify` answer (protected area, approved by the owner); a week 3 session without it is forgotten. Signed out, or the session over, the city of this device is played again; the account's waiting actions leave with their ids when the same account signs in again. The keep-or-replace question of week 3 is gone: after sign-in, one message if a city was played on this device. | The two cities never mix, and coming back to the first account loses no action. |
| 2026-10-16 | `POST /city/actions` checks the city with `parseState` after catching up the time and after the action: refused, nothing is written, the answer is `server_error`, and the log has the player id and the action type, never the city. | No answer carries, and the database never stores, a city the rules made invalid. |
| 2026-10-16 | The server applies actions: `POST /city/actions` (signed in), body `{ action }` read by `parseAction`, at most 2 KB. In one transaction the city is created if missing (`ensureCity`, the only function that creates one, also for `GET /city` from task 4), its row locked (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), advanced to the server's now, and the action applied with `applyAction`; the version goes up by 1 only when the city changed (reading a report already read keeps it). Table `applied_actions` keeps, per player and action id, only the outcome (applied, or refused with its code and reason) and a SHA-256 of the action, never the city, for 7 days. The same id with the same content gives the same outcome again, with the current city and `"replayed": true`; with other content, 409 `action_id_reused`. Every answer that reads the database carries the server's city `{ state, version, asOf, speed }`, plus `{ code, error }` on a refusal: 200, 422 `rule_refused` (the rule's reason word for word), 409 `action_id_reused`, 400 `invalid_action`. 413 `action_too_large` and 429 `too_many_actions` (60 actions per minute per player, counted before the body is read) never touch the database and carry no city: the client keeps its own and tries again. No city is written to the logs. | An action is applied exactly once, even when sent twice or at the same moment as another. The outcome is enough to answer a replay, since the current city is sent anyway; a stored city would only make the table grow. The limits protect the database: a flood or a huge body costs it nothing. |
| 2026-10-16 | `DEV_SPEED` is a server setting (1 by default) that speeds up only the game time of a city: the real seconds since its last save, times the speed. Sessions (30 days), nonces (5 min), rate-limit windows and the purges stay in real time. The server refuses to start with a `DEV_SPEED` other than 1 when `NODE_ENV=production`, and writes the speed in its log at start. `GET /city` and every action answer include `speed`. | CLAUDE.md rule 4: the rules stay in real seconds. A development shortcut must never shorten a session or a nonce, nor reach production unnoticed. |
| 2026-10-16 | A CI job `postgres` runs `server/test/concurrency.test.ts` on a real PostgreSQL 18.6 (a throwaway service database, `brinelight_test`). The test refuses a `TEST_DATABASE_URL` that is not on this machine (localhost, 127.0.0.1) or whose database name lacks "test". On PostgreSQL, another connection holds the city row until every action waits for it, so each action reads the city before any writes. Checked: without `FOR UPDATE`, three of its tests fail on every run (version 2 instead of 3, reports read then lost, a 500 for the same id sent twice). | PGlite runs one transaction at a time and cannot show a locking bug. Holding the row makes the worst case happen on every run, not by chance. |
| 2026-10-16 | A city played without an account stays on the device and is never imported to an account: a new account gets a new city made by the server (`newPlayerState`, through `ensureCity`), at its first action now and at its first `GET /city` from task 4, when `PUT /city` is removed. Guest accounts on the server can come with the embedded wallet (week 13). | That city was made by the client: importing it would let anyone edit their save, then sign in. |
| 2026-10-16 | Actions live in `shared/src/state/actions.ts`: a `GameAction` (`upgrade` a building, `sail` to a site, `readReport` by its id), each with a UUID made by the client. `applyAction` uses only the existing rules and never changes the city it receives; `parseAction` is strict (exact fields, UUID v4 in lowercase, known building, site or report id, bounded strings) and never throws. Every combat report gets a stable id `r1`, `r2`… from a counter in the city (`reportSeq`), never reused; reading a report gone or already read changes nothing, without a refusal. Old local saves get their report ids from `migrateSave`; `parseState` stays strict and needs them. A test checks that `shared/src` uses no clock and no randomness. | A new report can arrive between the player's tap and the server receiving the action, so the action names the report it read. A counter needs no clock and no randomness, and gives the same ids on the client and the server, however time is cut. |

| 2026-10-15 | Wallet sign-in (Sign In With Solana) is checked by the server alone, in `server/src/auth/`: the signature is verified with Node's own Ed25519 on the exact bytes the wallet returned, then the text is read strictly (each expected line once, no other line; `Expiration Time` required, at most 10 min after `Issued At`, which must be within 5 min of the server clock); domain, uri and the `Origin` header must match `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`; the chain is `SIWS_CHAIN_ID`. ## Open questions

- **Early game too slow at the current scale.** Refinery level 2 takes 54 real minutes, while the design document plans two very fast first hours. To be fixed in week 17 with a time curve specific to the first levels.
- **Lighthouse level 30.** Costs and durations beyond level 10 are extrapolated from the prototype. To be balanced by simulation in week 17.
- **When fish reaches 0 during a long absence, nothing else happens yet (survivors stay, morale does not drop).** To decide in week 17.
- **A wreck refills at once when emptied: add a delay?** To decide in week 17.
- **Camp levels are per player in the client; the server will own them in multiplayer.**
- **Two tabs offline at the same time share the account's copy on this device.** If one is closed before the network comes back, its waiting actions can be lost (actions already sent are safe, thanks to their ids). To look at if players use several tabs.
- **Changing the shape of the city once real cities are stored.** As soon as the server is rented (week 7 at the latest), each change of the city's shape (such as the research queue of week 4, task 6) will need the stored cities to be brought up to date, since `parseState` refuses a city with a missing field. How (a migration in the database, or a step when a city is read) is to be decided before renting the server.
