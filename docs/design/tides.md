# Tides

The design of the game's mode between players, approved by the owner on 21 October 2026. Its name in the game: **Tides**.

This is a design, not the game: no rule, no value and no code of the game changes with it. Every number below is a starting value approved by the owner, to be confirmed by simulation before it enters the game (see "Values to confirm").

## The principle

Every week, an alliance meets three alliances of its league on a small sea of 7 zones. Its members attack the other alliances' zones whenever they like, against the defense each player has recorded, with no loss at all: every troop comes home, nothing is stolen.

- **Asynchronous:** an attack is played against a recorded defense, never against a player who must be there at the same time.
- **At sea only:** a city can never be attacked (`CLAUDE.md`, "Game principles never to break"). Conflict between players happens only in Tides.
- **No loss:** a Tide is played for points, rewards and a week's harvest bonus, never for another player's troops, storage, buildings, plots or captains.

## Opening

- Tides open at **Lighthouse 8**.
- The first Tide is **guided**: the player is shown how to post a defense, choose a target and read a replay, against an alliance of the same league or a ghost alliance (see "Groups and leagues").

## The week

| Day | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | **Preparation.** The groups are matched. Each member posts one captain and one fleet on a zone held by their alliance: that is their recorded defense for the week. The week's weather is announced. |
| Tuesday to Thursday | **Attacks.** 3 attacks a day per player, at any hour. |
| Friday | **Results.** The zones held, the ranks, the rewards. |
| Saturday and Sunday | No Tide. |

## Groups and leagues

- A group is **4 alliances of the same league**, matched **across servers**.
- When a league has too few alliances to fill a group, it is completed with **ghost alliances**: their defenses are generated, never copied from real players.
- **Leagues by Lighthouse range.** In a Tide, troops are **brought to the league's level**, so that an alliance meets alliances of its own strength.

## The map

- **7 zones:** 6 edge zones (2 oil, 2 fish, 2 wood) and a center (Pearls, points doubled).
- Each alliance starts from an edge zone.
- A player can attack only a zone **next to a zone their alliance holds**.
- A zone falls when **all its defenses are beaten**.

## No loss

- All the troops come home after an attack, won or lost. Nothing is stolen.
- **Every attack earns points, even a lost one.** Attacking stronger earns more.

## Depth

What makes a Tide worth playing well:

- **The choice of defense:** which captain and which fleet to post, and on which zone.
- **The choice of target:** which zone and which defense to attack, and when.
- **The fleet's composition:** the troop types counter one another, so the fleet is built for the defense it meets.
- **A Tide talent for each captain**, which counts only in Tides.
- **The alliance's coordination:** which zones to take first, who attacks what, which defenses to break together.
- **One weather a week**, announced on Monday, which changes the best strategy.

## Replays

- Each attack has a **replay of about 15 seconds**, for the attacker and for the defender.

## Resources

- A zone **held at the end of the Tide** gives every member of the alliance **+15 % harvest of its resource** (oil, fish or wood) for **the following week**.
- That bonus **cannot be taken back** during that week.
- **The city's lands can never be attacked:** the bonus comes only from the Tide's zones.

## Rewards

- **A Tide chest for each participant**, from their first attack.
- **Alliance chests by rank** in the group.
- **Pearls to the Alliance Treasury** (which holds only Pearls and can never pay a chosen player).
- **League promotion and relegation.**
- **A season of 8 weeks:**
  - the title of **Guardians of the Great Lighthouse**, notarized on Solana (with no personal data on chain);
  - the season's **badges and cosmetics**.
- **Never a speed-up as a reward, never power for sale.** Tides attacks are never for sale (`CLAUDE.md`, "Never for sale").

## A welcome alliance

- A player without an alliance is placed in a **welcome alliance**, automatically, so that every player can take part in Tides.

## Values to confirm

All of these are starting values approved by the owner, to be confirmed by simulation before they enter the game:

- 3 attacks a day per player;
- the opening at Lighthouse 8;
- the zone bonus of +15 % for a week;
- the season of 8 weeks;
- the leagues (their Lighthouse ranges, the troops' level in each);
- the chests (what each one holds).

No value enters the game without the owner's agreement (`CLAUDE.md`, working rules).

## Not retained

- **Cities that can be attacked.** A city can never be attacked, looted or damaged by another player.
- **Real-time player-versus-player on the shared map.**
- **The depletion of the island's resources** (the study of PR #12).
